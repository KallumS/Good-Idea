# Session log - 2026-10-01

## What was asked

"The hardest thing in music is starting a track ... I want to create a
ReaScript that generates good ideas for when starting a new track": short
motifs, phrases or measures, exportable as MIDI. Three kinds - **Motif** (a
1-4 bar melody), **Phrase** (1-4 bars of melody, chords, or both in one
clip), **Measure** (8-16 bars of melody with chords and rhythmic material) -
and a **New Idea** button that makes one of the chosen kind at random, "not
based on a library of midi, but rather generated mathematically as music IS
math". In the house style of Starting Blocks, ScaleView for REAPER, Midi
Catalogue, Midi Suggester and Midi Variator, with every setting in the
window, a `CLAUDE.md`, decision records and this log.

The REAPER API functions page and the Educational Materials (Open Music
Theory among them) were uploaded alongside.

## What was done

**Read the sister repos first.** Midi Catalogue is the newest and closest,
so its shape was taken whole: the five-file layout (`Good Idea.lua`,
`gi_theory`, `gi_idea`, `gi_midi`, `gi_place`), the window's widgets and
colours, the MIDI writer, the placement code, the test harness and the
REAPER mock. ScaleView's keys and scales and Starting Blocks' chord table
came in unchanged, via Midi Catalogue. The dice are Midi Variator's.

**The one tension with the house.** Midi Catalogue's decision 0002 rejects
randomness; this request asks for it. The answer is
[0002](../decisions/0002-an-idea-is-a-number.md): every idea is a number, the
same number is always the same idea, and each part of an idea draws from its
own dice so a setting can change without losing the idea.

**The maths** ([0003](../decisions/0003-calculated-from-the-maths-of-music.md)):
the metric grid and Euclidean rhythms; a weighted walk through tonic,
subdominant and dominant (Open Music Theory's idealised phrase), with fixed
cadences; a weighted melodic walk pulled toward a contour whose arch peaks at
the golden section; and forms built from units - basic idea, repeat, answer,
sequence, fragment, cadence - after Open Music Theory's sentence and period
([0004](../decisions/0004-ideas-are-built-from-units.md)).

**Every setting in the window**, from one list, each with Any
([0005](../decisions/0005-every-setting-can-be-left-to-chance.md)): kind,
bars, content, key, scale, pace, groove, contour, register, chord colour,
chord pace, chord style, form, bass, drums, layout, velocity - plus the idea
number, back and forward, Keep, and Play new ideas.

**Decision records 0001-0009** and `CLAUDE.md`, `README.md`,
`docs/COLOUR.md` (the house scheme, plus the roll's new colours,
[0009](../decisions/0009-the-tune-in-the-accent.md)).

**The API check.** Every `reaper.` call was checked against the uploaded API
page (REAPER 7.79). All match; Good Idea calls nothing Midi Catalogue did not
already call, so the mock needed nothing new.

## What the demo found

Reading `tools/demo.lua` output, the house way, before trusting any test:

- Melodies stuttered (C5 C5 G4 G4) and trilled (C B C B). Repeats were made
  rarer, a third in a row forbidden, and a step back to the note before last
  discouraged.
- The "Mixed" colour put a flat ninth on iii (E G B F). Only a whole-tone
  ninth is added now.
- Pentatonic chords stacked from every other note came out as C E A. Scales
  other than seven notes now build chords by ear
  ([0006](../decisions/0006-chords-stay-in-the-key.md)).
- An answer phrase dropped its dominant (C C instead of G C), a Loop's chords
  did not repeat, an idea could open on V, and in 3/4 chords changed mid-bar.
- A "half cadence" could rest on IV. Half cadences now rest on dominants.

## What the tests found

The idea sweep, written to check every note of hundreds of ideas, failed on
its first run on twelve rules. Nearly all were real:

- **A copied ending is not an ending.** A repeat or sequence with a
  different cadence from its source copied the source's ending, so ideas
  meant to close on the tonic did not. Answers now copy their source's chords
  by time up to half way and walk to their own ending.
- **A closing note before its chord.** The tune came home a beat before the
  tonic chord did. A closing unit's last note now lands where the last chord
  arrives.
- **A one-note ending skipped the goal.** Fixed.
- **Half a bar of 3/4 copied past its end**, putting a note after the idea.
  The cut point is now always a beat before the end.
- **The fallback could repeat a chord or end a loop on the tonic** when the
  dice could not reach a cadence (Lydian's F#dim leads almost nowhere). It is
  now an exact search (Viterbi) for the likeliest path.
- **Syncopation barely showed**: rhythms were built half a bar at a time,
  and 3 in 4 twice is not a tresillo. Syncopated rhythms now spread over the
  whole bar.
- Test-side slips: `0.0` is not `"0"` as a table key; a `-` in a Lua find
  pattern; a bass test that ignored the snare taking the kick's place.

**The deep sweep.** At normal depth everything passed; at 12x and then 40x
(26,000 ideas) a handful of one-in-thousands cases appeared: a tune
stretching past two octaves in a pentatonic sequence, a note three times in
the diminished scale, a tritone the tidy pass could not move because every
alternative was out of range. The tidy pass (`I.untangle`) now runs twice,
looks wider when stuck, and keeps its octave fix in range; sequences choose
the least out-of-range transposition. `GOOD_IDEA_SWEEP=40` is now documented
as the check to run after any musical change.

**Proving the tests bite.** Thirteen deliberate breakages, twelve caught.
The one missed: handing the chords the melody's dice changed nothing any test
compared, because nothing compared the bass or drums under two chord styles.
A test does now, and chords and bass drawing from one stream fails it.

## Tried and abandoned

- **A pitch-anchored melody range** (centred on G4 whatever the key). It
  meant the same idea in another key was a different tune. The range is now
  counted from the tonic, and the test that a key change moves the same tune
  exists because of it.
- **Copying a repeat's chords by slot count.** Answers kept the first n/2
  chords even when the source's chords were twice as long, so the copied
  half of the tune no longer fitted. Copying by time replaced it.

## Numbers

`tools/test.sh`: 108 theory, 568,786 idea, 55 MIDI, 126 placement and 558
window checks. `GOOD_IDEA_SWEEP=40`: 21,741,555 idea checks, all passing.

## Left open

- **Not yet run inside REAPER.** Everything is tested against the mocks,
  which are written from the API page, but the window has not been seen.
  The first thing to do is open it, press New Idea a few times, insert a
  Measure and play it.
- `index.xml` points at the release commit; the ReaPack link works once the
  branch is merged into `main`.
- Chords are always in key: no borrowed chords or secondary dominants yet
  ([0006](../decisions/0006-chords-stay-in-the-key.md)).
- No swing, triplets or pickups (anacrusis); every idea starts on the
  downbeat.
- A Measure is 8, 12 or 16 bars, not every length between.
- The tempo is the project's; the idea does not ask for one. A tempo
  suggestion per pace might help when starting from nothing.
- Keep is all or nothing on Any; there is no way yet to keep the tune and
  re-roll only the chords under it.
- The time signature is read at the edit cursor; an idea does not follow a
  time signature change part way through.
