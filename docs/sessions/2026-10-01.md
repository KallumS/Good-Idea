# Session log - 2026-10-01

## What was asked

"The hardest thing in music is starting a track ... I want to create a
ReaScript that generates good ideas for when starting a new track": short
motifs, phrases or measures, exportable as MIDI. Three kinds - **Motif** (a
1-4 bar melody), **Phrase** (1-4 bars of melody, chords, or both in one
clip), **Measure** (8-16 bars of melody with chords and rhythmic material) -
and a **New Idea** button that makes one of the chosen kind at random, "not
based on a library of midi, but rather generated mathematically as music IS
math". In the house style of Starting Blocks, ScaleView for REAPER, Midi
Catalogue, Midi Suggester and Midi Variator, with every setting in the
window, a `CLAUDE.md`, decision records and this log.

The REAPER API functions page and the Educational Materials (Open Music
Theory among them) were uploaded alongside.

## What was done

**Read the sister repos first.** Midi Catalogue is the newest and closest,
so its shape was taken whole: the five-file layout (`Good Idea.lua`,
`gi_theory`, `gi_idea`, `gi_midi`, `gi_place`), the window's widgets and
colours, the MIDI writer, the placement code, the test harness and the
REAPER mock. ScaleView's keys and scales and Starting Blocks' chord table
came in unchanged, via Midi Catalogue. The dice are Midi Variator's.

**The one tension with the house.** Midi Catalogue's decision 0002 rejects
randomness; this request asks for it. The answer is
[0002](../decisions/0002-an-idea-is-a-number.md): every idea is a number, the
same number is always the same idea, and each part of an idea draws from its
own dice so a setting can change without losing the idea.

**The maths** ([0003](../decisions/0003-calculated-from-the-maths-of-music.md)):
the metric grid and Euclidean rhythms; a weighted walk through tonic,
subdominant and dominant (Open Music Theory's idealised phrase), with fixed
cadences; a weighted melodic walk pulled toward a contour whose arch peaks at
the golden section; and forms built from units - basic idea, repeat, answer,
sequence, fragment, cadence - after Open Music Theory's sentence and period
([0004](../decisions/0004-ideas-are-built-from-units.md)).

**Every setting in the window**, from one list, each with Any
([0005](../decisions/0005-every-setting-can-be-left-to-chance.md)): kind,
bars, content, key, scale, pace, groove, contour, register, chord colour,
chord pace, chord style, form, bass, drums, layout, velocity - plus the idea
number, back and forward, Keep, and Play new ideas.

**Decision records 0001-0009** and `CLAUDE.md`, `README.md`,
`docs/COLOUR.md` (the house scheme, plus the roll's new colours,
[0009](../decisions/0009-the-tune-in-the-accent.md)).

**The API check.** Every `reaper.` call was checked against the uploaded API
page (REAPER 7.79). All match; Good Idea calls nothing Midi Catalogue did not
already call, so the mock needed nothing new.

## What the demo found

Reading `tools/demo.lua` output, the house way, before trusting any test:

- Melodies stuttered (C5 C5 G4 G4) and trilled (C B C B). Repeats were made
  rarer, a third in a row forbidden, and a step back to the note before last
  discouraged.
- The "Mixed" colour put a flat ninth on iii (E G B F). Only a whole-tone
  ninth is added now.
- Pentatonic chords stacked from every other note came out as C E A. Scales
  other than seven notes now build chords by ear
  ([0006](../decisions/0006-chords-stay-in-the-key.md)).
- An answer phrase dropped its dominant (C C instead of G C), a Loop's chords
  did not repeat, an idea could open on V, and in 3/4 chords changed mid-bar.
- A "half cadence" could rest on IV. Half cadences now rest on dominants.

## What the tests found

The idea sweep, written to check every note of hundreds of ideas, failed on
its first run on twelve rules. Nearly all were real:

- **A copied ending is not an ending.** A repeat or sequence with a
  different cadence from its source copied the source's ending, so ideas
  meant to close on the tonic did not. Answers now copy their source's chords
  by time up to half way and walk to their own ending.
- **A closing note before its chord.** The tune came home a beat before the
  tonic chord did. A closing unit's last note now lands where the last chord
  arrives.
- **A one-note ending skipped the goal.** Fixed.
- **Half a bar of 3/4 copied past its end**, putting a note after the idea.
  The cut point is now always a beat before the end.
- **The fallback could repeat a chord or end a loop on the tonic** when the
  dice could not reach a cadence (Lydian's F#dim leads almost nowhere). It is
  now an exact search (Viterbi) for the likeliest path.
- **Syncopation barely showed**: rhythms were built half a bar at a time,
  and 3 in 4 twice is not a tresillo. Syncopated rhythms now spread over the
  whole bar.
- Test-side slips: `0.0` is not `"0"` as a table key; a `-` in a Lua find
  pattern; a bass test that ignored the snare taking the kick's place.

**The deep sweep.** At normal depth everything passed; at 12x and then 40x
(26,000 ideas) a handful of one-in-thousands cases appeared: a tune
stretching past two octaves in a pentatonic sequence, a note three times in
the diminished scale, a tritone the tidy pass could not move because every
alternative was out of range. The tidy pass (`I.untangle`) now runs twice,
looks wider when stuck, and keeps its octave fix in range; sequences choose
the least out-of-range transposition. `GOOD_IDEA_SWEEP=40` is now documented
as the check to run after any musical change.

**Proving the tests bite.** Thirteen deliberate breakages, twelve caught.
The one missed: handing the chords the melody's dice changed nothing any test
compared, because nothing compared the bass or drums under two chord styles.
A test does now, and chords and bass drawing from one stream fails it.

## Tried and abandoned

- **A pitch-anchored melody range** (centred on G4 whatever the key). It
  meant the same idea in another key was a different tune. The range is now
  counted from the tonic, and the test that a key change moves the same tune
  exists because of it.
- **Copying a repeat's chords by slot count.** Answers kept the first n/2
  chords even when the source's chords were twice as long, so the copied
  half of the tune no longer fitted. Copying by time replaced it.

## Numbers

`tools/test.sh`: 108 theory, 568,786 idea, 55 MIDI, 126 placement and 558
window checks. `GOOD_IDEA_SWEEP=40`: 21,741,555 idea checks, all passing.

## Left open

- **Not yet run inside REAPER.** Everything is tested against the mocks,
  which are written from the API page, but the window has not been seen.
  The first thing to do is open it, press New Idea a few times, insert a
  Measure and play it.
- `index.xml` points at the release commit; the ReaPack link works once the
  branch is merged into `main`.
- Chords are always in key: no borrowed chords or secondary dominants yet
  ([0006](../decisions/0006-chords-stay-in-the-key.md)).
- No swing, triplets or pickups (anacrusis); every idea starts on the
  downbeat.
- A Measure is 8, 12 or 16 bars, not every length between.
- The tempo is the project's; the idea does not ask for one. A tempo
  suggestion per pace might help when starting from nothing.
- Keep is all or nothing on Any; there is no way yet to keep the tune and
  re-roll only the chords under it.
- The time signature is read at the edit cursor; an idea does not follow a
  time signature change part way through.

# Later the same day - 1.1: rhythm and borrowed chords

## What was asked

"This is brilliant ... The only thing that comes to mind, it would be good if
there was a way to make the generations more rhythmically interesting - such
as chords and notes that don't start on beat, or have a chance to be a
triplet or dotted. Perhaps there could be a global swing slider too. If
there's a way to implement borrowed chords without breaking the script that
would be good too, but if you did this the borrowed chords should only
appear rarely in generations and the UI should flag when a borrowed chord
appears and which scale it's from."

## What was done

**Before touching anything**, thirty 1.0 ideas were hashed, so a test could
prove that with the new features off every 1.0 idea number still gives the
1.0 idea. That shaped everything after: the new settings went at the *end*
of the settings list (it is also the order the dice are drawn in), and each
new feature draws nothing when it is off.

**Rhythm** ([0010](../decisions/0010-figures-push-and-swing.md)), three
layers over what 1.0 makes:

- **Figures** - Plain, Dotted, Triplets, Mixed. Dotted eighths and
  sixteenths, dotted quarters and eighths, eighth-note and quarter-note
  triplets, now and then, in the tune and the Pulse chords; Broken chords
  roll in triplets and the hats shuffle. Triplet notes sit at fractional
  steps on the existing grid rather than a finer one.
- **Push** - None, Some, Lots. A chord change moves an eighth early; the
  tune's note, the kick and the bass come with it, and a held chord is not
  struck again on the beat. The chord line marks it `^`.
- **Swing** - a slider, 0-100%, applied last to every note on its way out.
  Not offered in 6/8, 12/8 or 7/8, and the window says why.

**Borrowed chords** ([0011](../decisions/0011-borrowed-chords-rarely-and-named.md)):
modal mixture only - the same degree from another seven-note scale on the
same key note. About one idea in four, one chord, never the opening or the
cadence. The tune bends to the borrowed scale: every place that turned a
scale position into a pitch now asks which scale is sounding at that moment.
The window flags it in the body text - "Borrowed chord: Ab (bVI) in bar 3,
from C Minor" - and the chord line marks it `*`.

## What the tests and the demo found

- **A borrowed chord with nothing borrowed in it.** Under the Mixed colour a
  "borrowed" chord could differ from the home chord only in its added note,
  all in key. Borrowing now needs a note from outside the key.
- **Broken triplets**, three ways: an answer copying half a statement cut a
  quarter-note triplet in two; a held closing note cut one off; a pushed note
  pulled a triplet's first note away from the other two. A last pass now
  keeps every triplet whole (an incomplete one goes back to straight), a push
  leaves a triplet's first note alone, and an ending is never a triplet note.
- **Mending a triplet made a note three times running** - so the triplet
  pass runs before the tidy pass, not after.
- **Swing skipped a note** whose end was a triplet's start; starts and ends
  now swing separately.
- **A repeat is not always over the same chords any more.** Borrowing and
  pushing touch one pass of the harmony, so a unit repeated exactly could put
  a note on the beat over a borrowed chord it did not fit, or end a loop on a
  note meant for a push that only came the first time. Exact repeats are now
  fitted to the chords under them, ending included - which changes nothing
  where the chords are the same, so 1.0 ideas are untouched. The deep sweep
  found this; normal depth did not, so a targeted test now covers it.
- **Notes that start together could be listed in a different order from run
  to run**: Lua 5.4 shuffles sorts of more than a hundred items, and a few
  sorts compared only start times. Every sort that can tie on a long list is
  now fully ordered. A tie-break on one short list was left as 1.0 had it -
  changing it changed two 1.0 ideas, and a list that short is sorted the
  same way every time. The 1.0 fingerprints were re-recorded from the 1.0
  code, hashed in sorted order so they cannot depend on it.
- Test-side: the triplet check first knew only eighth-note triplets; the
  swing check compared two ideas note by note in an order that is not
  guaranteed; the push check counted a note that was naturally on the "and".

**Proving the new tests bite**: sixteen deliberate breakages - the tune
reading the home key under a borrowed chord, borrowing at the ends, borrowing
often, a borrowed chord all in key, figures drawing when plain, the triplet
pass, triplets in 6/8, the kick, a pushed chord struck twice, the tune not
coming with the push, swing on triplets, swing in 6/8, swing's amount,
repeats not fitted, swing not saved, the flag missing. Fifteen caught at
once; repeats-not-fitted only at depth, and now by name too. The kick's
breakage showed up first as non-determinism, which is how the sorting
problem above was found.

## Numbers

`tools/test.sh`: 108 theory, about 568,000 idea, 55 MIDI, 118 placement and
646 window checks. `GOOD_IDEA_SWEEP=40`: 21,730,377 idea checks, all passing.

## Left open

- **Still not run inside REAPER.** The swing slider is ReaImGui's
  `SliderInt`, called exactly as Midi Variator calls its Amount slider
  (`"%d%%"`), so that call at least is proven there.
- No secondary dominants or other chromatic chords; borrowing is modal
  mixture only.
- Triplets and dotted figures only in quarter-note metres; 6/8 gets no
  duplets.
- Swing is the same for every part; there is no per-part swing, and no
  sixteenth-note swing.
- A borrowed chord comes in one pass of a loop, not every pass.

# Later still - 1.2: a shorter window, drums on their own, pull

## What was asked

"The UI is getting a bit long, could some of the sections open out or hidden
behind menus? Could we remove the drums toggle from the Measure section and
add it alongside Motif, Phrase and Measure. The new drum generator will make
drum patterns using general midi drum mapping. Drum generations to be 1 - 16
bars with the ability to create drum fills. I like the implementation of the
push feature - could we also add one for pull where the chords can arrive an
eighth late? Could we add 1.5 a bar to the chord pace? The figures section
... only seems to affect timing of the melody and not chords. Layout Tracks/
One Item should be at the bottom of the GUI alongside Export.mid and
Audition."

## What was done

- **The steps fold** ([0012](../decisions/0012-steps-fold-away.md)). Every
  step but Idea is a button with a one-line summary of its choices; click
  to open, click to fold. The first screen went from over eighty buttons to
  nineteen. Buttons inside, not drop-downs, as everywhere in the house.
- **Layout** moved down beside Insert, Export and Audition, on one row with
  Velocity.
- **Drums is a fourth kind** ([0013](../decisions/0013-drums-are-a-kind-of-idea.md)):
  1 to 16 bars, a Beat (backbeat, half-time, four on the floor, breakbeat),
  Fills (none, at the end, every 4 bars, every 2 bars), and a Cymbal (hats
  or ride). Grooves in pairs of bars, the second answering the first; fills
  of snare rolls, tom runs, both, or triplets; a crash on the downbeat each
  fill lands on, the top of the idea for the last. The roll draws a drum
  idea in lanes.
- **The Measure's drums switch is retired** - a Measure always has drums. It
  stays, invisible and always On, in the settings list, because removing it
  would have shifted the dice for every setting after it.
- **Pull** ([0014](../decisions/0014-pull-the-chords-lie-back.md)): chords
  an eighth late, but only the chords part - the tune, the bass and the
  drums stay on the beat. Done to a copy of the harmony the chords part
  alone plays from. Marked `_` in the chord line.
- **1.5 a bar** ([0015](../decisions/0015-chord-rhythm-figures-and-one-and-a-half.md)):
  three chords to two bars, 3+3+2 beats. Any does not roll it, so 1.0's
  numbers keep their chord pace.
- **Figures in the chords**: the user was right - Block chords, the
  commonest style, ignored them entirely. Now Block chords take stabs (a
  dotted quarter in, or a quarter-note triplet), Broken arpeggios go
  long-short as well as rolling in triplets, and a Moving bass takes them
  too.

## What the tests found

- **Four on the floor stopped being four on the floor**: dotted figures were
  laid over its kick. They no longer are.
- **A pulled chord inside a triplet arpeggio** struck two thirds of a
  sixteenth before the arpeggio's next note, and swing then put the two on
  the same moment. A pulled chord now keeps a sixteenth clear behind it.
- **An open hi-hat rang past the end** of a 7/8 drum idea (the deep sweep,
  four notes in 4.5 million). Drum notes now stop at the end.
- The 1.0 regression check needed Pull on None too: a new setting on Any is
  a new feature on.
- The window tests needed to open the steps they reach into; a helper now
  opens the step a setting lives in, and the sweep clicks every button with
  the steps folded and again with them open.

**Proving the new tests bite**: fifteen breakages - pull moving the tune,
strokes before a pull, the held chord, 1.5 a bar in Any, block stabs, the
figured bass, fills misplaced, the crash, the answering bar, the drum
channel, the retired setting removed, folding, the layout row, the Drums
step, and pull drawing dice when off. Fourteen caught. The last cannot
matter: pull has a dice stream of its own, so an extra draw there changes
nothing else.

## Numbers

`tools/test.sh`: 108 theory, about 745,000 idea, 55 MIDI, 118 placement and
529 window checks. `GOOD_IDEA_SWEEP=40`: 28,577,339 idea checks, all
passing.

## Left open

- Still not run inside REAPER.
- Folded or open is not remembered between sessions (the house's rule for
  the view); if opening the same steps every time gets tiresome, that is a
  small change.
- Drum ideas have no ghost notes (velocity is 100 or 115, the house rule)
  and no per-drum choices (which toms, which crash).
- Pull moves the chords only; a bass that lies back too would be another
  setting.

# Last - 1.3: a Measure without drums; chord paces in numbers

## What was asked

"Could we remove drums entirely from Measure. Could we also add 0.5 a bar
and 4 a bar to the chord paces please."

## What was done

- **A Measure is melody, chords and bass**
  ([0016](../decisions/0016-a-measure-has-no-drums-paces-in-numbers.md)).
  The Measure's drum part (`drumsPart`) is gone; drums are the Drums kind's
  alone. On Tracks a Measure makes three tracks; in one item, channels 1 to
  3. The roll's drum strip went with it.
- **Nothing else in a Measure changed.** A Pulse bass plays the kick
  drum's pattern, and that pattern is still drawn from the `drums` dice
  stream, so every Measure's tune, chords and bass are note for note what
  they were.
- The retired `drums` switch now holds Off; an old saved `drums=On` comes
  back Off.
- **The chord pace reads 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 4 a bar.** "Slow" already was half
  a chord a bar (a chord every two bars), so it was relabelled rather than
  doubled; One and Two read 1 and 2. The stored names are unchanged, so
  saved settings and idea numbers still mean what they did.
- **4 a bar** is a chord on every beat: four a bar in 4/4, three in 3/4,
  two in 6/8. Like 1.5, it is chosen, never rolled by Any.

## What the tests found

- The 1.0 regression check's Measures had to change: their drums are gone.
  Their fingerprints were made again by the 1.0 code (872be29), leaving out
  channel 10. Motifs and Phrases kept theirs; all thirty pass.
- The tests that read a Measure's drums were turned round: the push test
  now checks the bass arrives with a pushed chord (it checked the kick),
  the Pulse bass test reads the kick pattern from the dice stream, and the
  separate-dice test compares tune and chords under two basses.
- The demo at 4 a bar reads well: C G C Dm | G F G C ... in 4/4, every beat
  a chord, the tune still on the chord on each beat, and the sentence's
  continuation no faster than the pace.

**Proving the new tests bite**: nine breakages - drums back in a Measure
(caught three times: idea, window, placement), Any rolling 4 a bar, 4 a bar
at 2, the kick pattern from another stream, the retired switch On, the
labels gone, a held bass ignoring a push. All nine caught.

## Numbers

`tools/test.sh`: 108 theory, 571,079 idea, 55 MIDI, 118 placement and 532
window checks. `GOOD_IDEA_SWEEP=40`: 21,724,517 idea checks, all passing
(fewer than 1.2's 28.6 million: a Measure has no drums to check).

## Left open

- Still not run inside REAPER.
- A drum idea and a Measure are separate ideas: nothing yet makes a groove
  to match a particular Measure's kick-and-bass pattern.

# Then - 1.4: figures in the tune and the chords together

## What was asked

"The figures section with Any/ Plain/ Dotted/ Triplets/ Mixed options now
only seems to affect timing of the chords and not melody (opposite to how it
was working before) - is it possible for it to affect the timing of both at
the same time without breaking the script?"

## What was found

The tune still took figures, but rarely. Over 300 ideas of each kind with
Triplets, about one beat in seven of the tune was a triplet, against nearly
two in five of the chords, and a third of Motifs and Phrases had no figure
in the tune at all. A figure fell only on two eighths in a beat or two
quarters across two beats. Tallying the tune's beats showed why: at Calm
and Flowing they are mostly lone quarter notes (and at Calm, half notes),
which neither shape touched. The figures that were made all survived to the
finished tune; the shortfall was in what could be figured at all.

## What was done

- In the tune only (`I.figure`'s new `tune` flag, set by `I.cell`), a
  quarter note becomes a dotted eighth and a sixteenth or an eighth-note
  triplet, and a half note a dotted quarter and an eighth or a quarter-note
  triplet ([0017](../decisions/0017-the-tune-takes-figures-too.md)). The
  odds were raised once after measuring: the first try took the tune from
  15% to 21% of beats in triplets; the second, to about 25%.
- What is left untouched on purpose: notes held over the bar, a Calm tune's
  long notes with nothing after them, and syncopated rhythms (their 3+3+2
  is a figure of its own). Most tunes still without a figure are Calm.
- The chords', bass's and drums' rhythm is exactly 1.3's (compared over 900
  figured ideas). The chords are voiced under the tune's lowest note, so in
  about one idea in nine they sit a little differently.

## What the demo showed

With Triplets, Flowing: "G4 A4@1.33 B4@1.67 C5" - a triplet run up into the
beat - and "C5 A4@1.33 G4@1.67 F4 E4@2.33 D4@2.67 C4", two triplets falling
home. With Dotted: "C5 D5@1.75 G4", the long-short lilt on the second beat.

## Numbers

`tools/test.sh`: 108 theory, 576,417 idea, 55 MIDI, 118 placement and 532
window checks. `GOOD_IDEA_SWEEP=40`: 21,921,596 idea checks, all passing.
The new tests (each new shape exactly; Plain and the chords untouched by
it; tune and chords figured together in most phrases; a fifth of the tune's
beats triplets) were run against the 1.3 engine: it fails four of the six
checks, all but the two that say what must not change.

## Left open

- The tune's and the chords' figures are drawn separately, so with Mixed a
  dotted tune can sit over a triplet in the chords on the same beat - a
  rub some will like and some will not. Matching them would be a setting.
